REQUIRED ACTIONS:

   - Click "Create Token"
   - Copy the generated token
   
2. Update HA_URL in clawdbot.json:
   Replace: http://homeassistant.tailscale-name.ts.net:8123

   Replace: YOUR_HA_LONG_LIVED_TOKEN_HERE
   With:    The long-lived token from step 1
